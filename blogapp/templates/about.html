About this paper

Two editions,
one desk.

ARNΛV is a small, independent publication written by Arnav Sharma: two editions, one desk, and no algorithm between you and the page.

There are two editions. The Devlog is the technical one: build notes, systems work, post-mortems, things that broke at 2 a.m. and the occasional graph. The Journal is the personal one: field notes, photographs, small essays and the slower parts of a life spent online.

They share a printing press but not a mood. The Devlog is set in dark ink and burnt orange; the Journal is warm paper and brown. Both are arranged like a newspaper, because a front page asks you to choose what to read instead of feeding you an infinite column.

The shape of it

There are two editions and one archive. The front page asks you to choose what to read; it ends. Every dispatch is searchable, and each one can be taken with you as a file or read in your own reader. There are no comments and no tracking pixels — only a view count that forgets who you are after the day is over.

Take it with you

Every dispatch can be downloaded as Markdown, HTML, EPUB, plain text or JSON, and the print view becomes a PDF in one click. The feeds are open: Devlog RSS and Journal RSS.

The editor

The editor's desk is at /admin. It signs in through GitHub as the paper's owner and nothing else. If you are here to read, you are in the right place.
